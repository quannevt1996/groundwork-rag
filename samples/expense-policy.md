# Travel and Expense Policy

Fernbrook Labs reimburses reasonable costs you incur while working. This policy explains what is covered, what needs approval first and how to get paid back.

## Approval limits

- Expenses under 100 EUR do not need approval in advance.
- Expenses from 100 EUR up to 1,000 EUR need approval from your manager before you spend the money.
- Anything above 1,000 EUR needs approval from a director. Your manager escalates the request; you do not need to contact the director yourself.

Splitting one purchase into several smaller claims to stay under a limit is treated as a policy violation.

## Travel

Book flights in economy class. For flights longer than six hours you may book premium economy. Business class is not reimbursed, whatever the length of the flight.

Hotels are reimbursed up to 180 EUR per night. In London, New York and Zurich the limit is 250 EUR per night.

When you travel for work you receive a per diem of 45 EUR per day for meals instead of submitting individual meal receipts.

## Home office

Everyone who works from home at least two days a week can claim a one-time home office stipend of 500 EUR for a desk, chair or monitor, plus a monthly internet allowance of 30 EUR.

## Submitting claims

Submit expense claims in Ledgerly within 60 days of the purchase. Claims submitted later are only paid with a director's approval. Attach a receipt for anything over 25 EUR. Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly salary.
