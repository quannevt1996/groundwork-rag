# New Engineer Onboarding

Welcome to Fernbrook Labs. This guide covers your first two months on the Wayline team.

## Your first week

Your laptop arrives already enrolled in device management. On your first day, request access to the systems you need through Gatehouse, our access request tool. Most requests are approved by your manager within a day.

Production access works differently. It requires a hardware security key, your manager's approval and a completed security training. Finish the security training within your first 14 days; it takes about two hours.

## Your onboarding buddy

Every new engineer is paired with an onboarding buddy from another team for their first six weeks. Your buddy is the person to ask the questions you think are too small to ask anyone else. Expect a 30-minute check-in with them twice a week.

## Your first deploy

We want you to ship a small change to production within your first two weeks. It does not matter how small it is; the point is to go through code review, CI and a production deploy once with someone beside you.

## Joining the on-call rotation

New engineers do not carry the pager straight away. First you shadow two full rotations, joining every page and handover as an observer. After that you can join the primary rotation, at the earliest in your eighth week. Your manager and the current primary decide together when you are ready.
